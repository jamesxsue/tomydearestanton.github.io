Dear Anton

Hey baby,

As the days went by, every single time we happened to be in the same room, my eyes would instantly hunt for you.

I kept stupidly expecting you to run up to me, throw your arms around me, and start talking to me like nothing had changed.

But whenever you’d just walk past me without even a second glance, acting like I was a complete stranger…

god, Anton, I’d be lying if I said it didn’t sting.

Standing in the exact same room as you, feeling that icy distance between us, absolute ate me alive.

It drove me completely insane, especially seeing you around Sion.

Watching you smile at him, tease him, and give him that exact same gentle, radiant warmth you used to reserve just for me...

it broke something inside me.

I was losing my mind, realizing that the warmth I threw away was now keeping someone else safe.

The truth is, I grew up with internalized homophobia running far too deep.

Deep down, I always knew I wasn’t the ideal son my father wanted me to be, and that made me deeply, endlessly insecure.

I conditioned myself to believe that these intense feelings I had whenever I was with you were wrong—that love like this was a mistake, a flaw, something dirty I needed to run away from.

Feeling something I was taught my whole life to dislike terrified me to my absolute core.

I had never felt anything remotely like this before, and realizing that I was falling deeply in love with you only amplified my insecurity and my sickening fear of disappointing my dad.

But realizing that all these terrifying feelings were for you... somehow made everything better.

You made me feel grounded and certain about something I had spent my entire life cowering from.

Even when the whole world felt wrong, being near you made my fears go quiet.

You gave me a soft place to land, showing me a kind of tenderness and unconditional safety I never thought someone like me was allowed to feel.

I am so, so sorry for taking your heart for granted, for thoroughly confusing you, and for causing you so much unnecessary pain just because I was too much of a coward to face my own truth sooner.

You deserved absolute honesty and gentle love, and instead, I gave you my unresolved baggage and defensive pride.

I know right now you’re not ready to face me or look at me, and that’s completely okay.

I fully respect your boundaries and your space.

Basta, please just know that I’m always going to be right here, waiting patiently with open arms, just in case you ever decide you want to run back into them.
